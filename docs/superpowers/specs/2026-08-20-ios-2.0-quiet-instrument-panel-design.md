## 1. What this is

A design-token sheet and screen inventory for the iOS 2.0 redesign, in **two themes**:

- **Dark Instrument** (default) — technical precision, thin borders, mono for spec
  values, one loud accent on near-black.
- **Daylight Workbench** — *a re-ink, not an inversion*. Warm paper canvas, true-white
  cards, hairlines carrying depth, every readable colour re-anchored to clear AA.

Tokens + inventory only. No per-screen layout specs. Two product rules from the
ratified platform spec bind the redesign and are repeated as pointers, not new
decisions:

- A gear pre-fills **hardware only**. Intent stays per print — no "Generate Profile
  for [gear]" CTA. (Platform spec §2.)
- **Workshop is star-save-from-Output, not a browsable catalog.** Confirmed against
  `Strings.Workshop.emptyMessage`.

**Three load-bearing rules, not preferences:**

1. **`ctaFill` is FILL-ONLY in light.** `#00E5A0` as text, icon, thin border or status
   dot on white measures **1.65:1** — invisible. It is legal only as a filled shape
   carrying `onPrimary #04231A` (10.09:1). Anything green that must *read* as a mark
   uses `signalMark #009468`.
2. **`textTertiary` may never carry required reading** — in either theme. Dark ≈3.2:1,
   light 4.05/3.95. Decorative and meta only.
3. **`primaryRamp` takes real values in light, not percentages** — the dark 10%/5%
   washes vanish on white:
   `100 → #007A56` text / `#009468` mark · `45 → rgba(0,148,104,0.50)` border ·
   `10 → rgba(0,148,104,0.12)` fill · `5 → rgba(0,148,104,0.07)` wash.

**Dark-only tokens:** the warning/info text tints `#FF8A5E` and `#A89DFA` exist to lift
those hues off near-black. Light uses the base hues `#BF4515` / `#5648D4` directly.

> **Cross-surface action, from the canvas itself:** the reconciled light values should
> be **shipped back to the web `[data-theme="light"]` theme**. The shipped web green
> `#009a6a` measures **3.60:1** on white and fails AA today. See §8.5.

> **Standing iOS rule, not from the canvas:** press animations come from a
> `ButtonStyle`'s `configuration.isPressed`. A `DragGesture(minimumDistance: 0)`
> inside a `ScrollView` starves the pan recognizer and silently kills scrolling —
> this shipped as a live bug in 1.1.3. `EdgeSwipeBack.swift` is UIKit-backed on
> purpose; do not "simplify" it into a SwiftUI gesture.

## 6. SwiftUI mapping and theming architecture

From the canvas:

- **One `Theme` struct, two instances.** Colors as `Color(hex:)` statics; ramps via
  `.opacity()` on primary.
- **`Auto` follows the system** — `@Environment(\.colorScheme)`. `@AppStorage` stores
  only an **explicit override**, and `Auto` is the default for new installs.
- The Appearance sheet (`4b`) offers three options: **Dark Instrument · Daylight
  Workbench · Auto**.
- Fonts: `Font.custom("Syne", size:)` / `Font.custom("DMMono-Medium", size:)` / system
  for body. **Conflicts with the shipped helpers — see §8.3.**
- Radii: `.clipShape(.rect(cornerRadius: r, style: .continuous))`.

## 8. Delta vs. the shipped iOS app

Measured against `3dprintassistant-ios` at `1e3de11`.

### 8.1 Dark colors — no migration needed

`ColorTheme.swift` already carries the exact dark palette: `primary #00e5a0`,
`warning #ff6b35`, `info #7b6ef6`, `background #0a0a0b`, `surface #111114`,
`surface2 #18181d`. The redesign confirmed the palette rather than replacing it.

### 8.2 Dark colors — four tokens are new

Absent from `ColorTheme.swift`: `onPrimary #04231A`, `news/link #4ED6FF`, the text
tints `#FF8A5E` / `#A89DFA`, and the named `primaryRamp` steps (today only a single
`primaryDim` at 0.12).

`textSecondary` ships as `Color(white: 0.6)` and `textTertiary` as `Color(white: 0.3)`
— matching the sheet's 60% / 35% (**§9.2, resolved**).

### 8.3 Fonts — Syne and DM Mono have never rendered

**Largest delta, and it predates the redesign.**

- `Font.syne(_:weight:)` / `Font.dmMono(_:)` are defined at
  [`ColorTheme.swift:35-42`](../../../../3dprintassistant-ios/3DPrintAssistant/Utils/ColorTheme.swift:35)
  and called from **17 sites across 11 view files**.
- The repo contains **zero** font files (`find . \( -iname '*.ttf' -o -iname '*.otf' \)` → 0).
- There is **no `UIAppFonts`** key in any plist, pbxproj or xcconfig.
- `Font.custom` with an unregistered name falls back to the system font. The source
  comment admits it: *"falls back to system if Syne isn't bundled yet."*

Every Syne and DM Mono call site in the shipping app renders **San Francisco**. The
typographic half of "Quiet Instrument Panel" — which leans on mono for spec values —
is not currently deliverable. **§3 is unimplementable until this is fixed.**

*Same shape as the 2026-08-18 nozzle-picker bug: correct call sites, nothing verifying
what the user actually sees.*

### 8.4 Light mode is actively forbidden, and ~49 sites are hardcoded

- **`.preferredColorScheme(.dark)` appears 6 times**, including on the root container
  at [`ContentView.swift:80`](../../../../3dprintassistant-ios/3DPrintAssistant/App/ContentView.swift:80),
  forcing the whole app dark regardless of system setting. Also in
  `ProductUpdatesView`, `WarningsSheet`, `FeedbackView`, `ChecklistSheet`, `TipJarSheet`.
- The app owns **zero** asset-catalog color sets — every `.colorset` in the repo belongs
  to the Sentry dependency. `ColorTheme` is flat statics with nowhere to put a light value.
- **49 hardcoded `.white` / `.black` / `Color.white` / `Color.black` occurrences across
  14 view files**, led by `OutputView` (5), `FeedbackView` (5), `SharedComponents` (5),
  `TipJarSheet` (4), `HomeView` (4), `PrinterPickerView` (4), `MaterialPickerView` (4).
  Each renders wrong or invisible on a light background — e.g.
  `WorkshopView.swift:55` is a toast with `.foregroundStyle(.white)` over `surface2`,
  which in light becomes white text on `#FAFBF8`.

**Light mode is therefore a migration task, not a palette swap:** remove the 6 dark
locks, make `ColorTheme` scheme-aware, migrate ~49 call sites. It should be scheduled
as its own task, never folded into a UI task.

`elevated` needing no visual separation (`#FAFBF8` is 1.04:1 from `#FFFFFF`) is **not**
a problem: all three `surface2` usages — a toast capsule, a pill button, a tip-tier card
— already draw an explicit border over the fill, and none signals selection. Selection
is the green border. Sheets carry their own shadow. **Owner call 2026-08-20: no action.**

### 8.5 Web light theme fails AA today — spillover finding

`style.css:32-56` ships `html[data-theme="light"]`. Measured on white:
green `#009a6a` **3.60:1** ❌ · orange `#d04a14` **4.50:1** ✅ · purple `#5648d4`
**6.42:1** ✅ · link `#0b7fc4` **4.33:1** ❌.

Two of four fail AA body text on the live site. §2's reconciled values fix both and
would make web and iOS agree for the first time. **Web-only, independent of Train 1** —
file as its own item.

## 9. Decisions

1. **RESOLVED 2026-08-20 — `1i`, the utility brand picker.** Plain and calmer over
   brand-identity. **`1h` is superseded; do not build it.** This also closes the
   canvas's own trademark flag ("brand logotypes as styled `Text` today — real vector
   marks need trademark care before shipping"): the utility treatment carries no
   logotypes, so no trademark work is required before shipping. Unblocks Train 1 iOS
   task 9 (pickers).
2. **RESOLVED 2026-08-20 — `textSecond` = 60%.** The canvas was internally inconsistent
   (`4c` said 60%, `TOKENS`/`CRIT` said 55%). Owner chose the value the app already
   ships (`Color(white: 0.6)`). **60% is canonical; treat the 55% references as stale.**
3. **RESOLVED 2026-08-20 — `elevated` low separation: no action.** Rationale in §8.4.
4. **RESOLVED 2026-08-20 — bundle Syne + DM Mono.** Owner asked for the simpler path;
   bundling *is* the simpler path, because shipping on the system font would require
   re-specifying §3's whole type scale in SF — more work than adding two files, and it
   would cost the mono spec-readout that the "instrument panel" direction rests on.

   **Licensing verified from the licence files themselves** (`google/fonts`
   `ofl/syne/OFL.txt` and `ofl/dmmono/OFL.txt`, fetched 2026-08-20), not from memory:
   both are **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (`license: "OFL"` in each `METADATA.pb`).
   OFL 1.1 permits embedding in software including commercial apps. No cost, no
   in-app attribution requirement, no legal review needed.

   **Scope — schedule as its own task, before any UI task consumes §3:**
   (a) add the static instances to the Xcode project — Syne ships upstream as a
   variable font (`Syne[wght].ttf`), so prefer the `static/` instances to avoid
   variable-axis naming problems on iOS; (b) add `UIAppFonts` to Info.plist (no such
   key exists today); (c) reconcile the PostScript names — the shipped helpers use
   `"Syne-Bold"` / `"DMMonoRegular"`, the canvas specifies `"Syne"` /
   `"DMMono-Medium"`, and the actual font files are the authority; (d) verify on a
   real screen, not a unit test — the current failure mode is silent fallback, which
   no test catches. This retroactively fixes the 17 existing call sites in §8.3.
5. **OPEN (owner confirmation only) — `3DPA 2.0 Flow.dc.html` is empty.** The Claude
   Design project carries two pages; the second contains a bare `<x-dc></x-dc>` and no
   artboards. Re-read 2026-08-20, still empty. Nothing depends on it — the question is
   only whether flow work was drawn there and failed to save. If the page was never
   used, mark it unused and close this item.

## 10. Accessibility

### 10.1 Contrast — independently verified this session

Recomputed from the WCAG relative-luminance formula. **Every token clears its bar
against both light backgrounds.** Where the canvas's own figures differ they
*under*-claim — `textSecond` is stated 5.1/4.5 but measures 6.31/6.03, `textTertiary`
stated 3.8/3.4 but measures 4.05/3.95. Erring safe.

Dark-theme ratios in §10.2 are the designer's, **not** recomputed here.

### 10.2 Designer self-critique — *verbatim claims, not verified*

**Dark contrast (claimed):** `#00E5A0` on `#0A0A0B` → 12.0:1 · `#04231A` on `#00E5A0`
→ 10.2:1 · white 55% on `#111114` → ≈6.5:1 · `#FF6B35` → 7.0:1 · `#7B6EF6` → 5.1:1
(badge/large only). Self-flagged: white 35% ≈3.2:1, decorative only; `#4ED6FF` links
≈8:1 but small at 10pt mono — keep ≥10pt medium.

**Tap targets:** tab items ~94×54pt ✓ · gear rows 56pt ✓ · CTA 56pt ✓ · brand cells
50pt visual → pad to 44pt+ via `contentShape` · **intent chips 34–36pt visual — below
44pt**, extend hit rect vertically (`contentShape` + 6pt) · give each footer link a 44pt
hit zone.

**Hierarchy & Danish (+20% length):** Home carries six modules, only two loud (gear card,
CTA). Risk: the Support card's green wash competes with the gear card's green border —
drop to green/3% if it reads loud in build. Cap gear names at **20 chars at creation** so
the CTA sub-line never truncates. "Quality above all" → **"Quality"** to survive
"Kvalitet".

**SwiftUI friction predicted:** custom tab bar required (`TabView` can't draw the tick)
— `safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)` over a router enum. Overlay = sheet +
`presentationDetents([.large])` + `LazyVStack(pinnedViews:)` for printer headers.
Gear→wizard handoff via `matchedGeometryEffect` — **test on device, it can stutter inside
sheets; fallback crossfade.** Staggers capped at 8. Continuous corners are native.
