13DPA 2.0 — Quiet Instrument Panel
BRIEFAssumptions & decisions — read first
ASSUMPTIONS
Engine is local and offline (privacy-first). Credit purchase and Pro checkout flows are out of scope this round — only their surfaces are designed. Mocks are English; every layout leaves ~20% headroom for Danish. Canvas is 402×874 pt (iPhone 16 Pro class).
PALETTE — KEEP #00E5A0, NO SECOND PALETTE
Green stays the sole anchor, formalized as a ramp: 100 signal · 45 border · 10 fill · 5 wash. Each secondary gets one job: #7B6EF6 = Pro/paid, #4ED6FF = news/new items, #FF6B35 = warnings only. An evolved palette would fragment what each color means on an instrument panel — argued against, not shown.
SIX DECISIONS
1 · Four tabs, not five. Home · Expert · Inventory · Workshop. Feedback is an act, not a place — it moves to the Home footer and the profile screen. Danish labels stay one word: Hjem · Ekspert · Lager · Værksted.
2 · CTA semantics. The Home CTA reads “Configure print” with the gear named beneath it — it continues with hardware, it never says Generate. Intent is per print.
3 · Gear legibility. The nozzle badge (size + type) is the visual key and material is co-primary; printer name is secondary. The overlay groups by printer, so duplicates read by their differences.
4 · The brand mark. The benchy stays — flattened to a vector glyph in a 34pt tile. The two-line Syne title survives only on the first-run hero, where it is the welcome mat; returning Home gets the compact wordmark + a live engine-status readout.
5 · Gears are born in two places. Quick-save banner on the profile screen (1l) and a manage list behind “Edit” in the overlay (1g).
6 · Monetization, premium-quiet. One combined Support & Pro card on Home, a PRO badge on the Inventory tile, a credit count on the Expert tile. Nothing pulses, nothing counts down.

E — SELF-CRITIQUE + TOKEN SHEET
CRITSelf-critique — contrast, targets, hierarchy, SwiftUI friction
CONTRAST (COMPUTED, WCAG)
#00E5A0 on #0A0A0B → 12.0:1 ✓ · #04231A on #00E5A0 → 10.2:1 ✓ · white 55% on #111114 → ≈6.5:1 ✓ · #FF6B35 on bg → 7.0:1 ✓ · #7B6EF6 on bg → 5.1:1 ✓ (badge/large only)
Two failures I’m flagging: white 35% footer/meta text ≈ 3.2:1 — legal only because it is tertiary/decorative; anything a user must read sits at ≥50% (≈5.5:1). And #4ED6FF “MANAGE/EDIT” links ≈ 8:1 ✓ but at 10pt mono they are small — keep ≥10pt medium.
CONTRAST — DAYLIGHT WORKBENCH (COMPUTED, VS #FFFFFF / #F2F3EF)
#101613 → 18.2 / 16.3 ✓ · ink 68% → 5.1 / 4.5 ✓ · #007A56 → 5.4 / 4.8 ✓ · #BF4515 → 5.1 / 4.6 ✓ · #077099 → 5.6 / 5.0 ✓ · #5648D4 → 6.4 / 5.7 ✓ · #04231A on #00E5A0 → 10.1 ✓
Flagged in light: #00E5A0 as text, border or dot on white is 1.65:1 — banned outside fills (CTA FILL-ONLY rule); marks re-ink to #009468 (3.9 / 3.5), clearing the 3:1 non-text floor but never carrying text; tertiary ink 55% is 3.8 / 3.4 — decorative-only, the same contract as dark's white 35%.
TAP TARGETS
Tab items ~94×54pt ✓ · gear rows 56pt ✓ · CTA 56pt ✓ · brand cells 50pt visual → pad hit area to 44pt+ via contentShape ✓ · intent chips 34–36pt visual — below 44pt: extend hit rect vertically (contentShape + 6pt) · footer links are small by design; give the whole footer row a 44pt hit zone per link.
HIERARCHY & DANISH
Home carries six modules; only two are visually loud (gear card, CTA) — the rest sit at ≤55% white. Risk: the Support card’s green wash competes with the gear card’s green border; if it reads too loud in build, drop its wash to green/3%. Danish +20%: chips wrap (“Funktionel del” fits), CTA sub-line uses the gear name — cap gear names at 20 chars at creation so nothing ever truncates. “Quality above all” → “Quality” to survive DA (“Kvalitet”).
SWIFTUI FRICTION
Custom tab bar required (TabView can’t draw the tick) — use safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom) over a router enum. Overlay = sheet + presentationDetents([.large]) + LazyVStack(pinnedViews:) for printer headers. Gear→wizard summary handoff = matchedGeometryEffect (test on device; it can stutter inside sheets — fallback: crossfade). Staggers = .delay(Double(i) * 0.03), cap at 8 rows. Continuous corners are native. Brand logotypes as styled Text today — real vector marks need trademark care before shipping.
